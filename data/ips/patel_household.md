# Investment Policy Statement

**Clients:** Priya Patel and Arjun Patel
**Prepared by:** Marcus Hale, CFP, Cedar Ridge Wealth Partners (the "Firm")
**Effective date:** April 7, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This Investment Policy Statement ("IPS") describes how the Firm will manage the investment assets of Priya and Arjun Patel.

1.2 This IPS applies to the following accounts (together, the "Portfolio"):

- the Patel joint brokerage account at Pinnacle Custody;
- Priya's Kestrel Semiconductor equity plan account at Northstar Plan Services, which the Firm manages under a limited trading authorization;
- Arjun's rollover IRA at Pinnacle Custody.

1.3 The Patels keep an emergency reserve equal to about six months of household expenses in a savings account at their bank. That reserve is outside the scope of this IPS and the Firm does not manage it.

## 2. Objectives and Risk Profile

2.1 Priya (41) is a Vice President of Engineering at Kestrel Semiconductor. Arjun (43) is a product designer and works as an independent consultant. They have two children, ages 9 and 6.

2.2 The Patels' primary objective is long-term growth of capital. They hope to have the flexibility to stop working full-time by about age 55. They also plan to pay for most of their children's college costs and fund 529 plans separately.

2.3 The Patels have a high tolerance for risk and a time horizon of more than fifteen years. They do not expect to withdraw from the Portfolio in the next ten years, other than to pay taxes on vesting equity awards.

## 3. Asset Allocation

3.1 The Portfolio will be managed within the following ranges, measured at market value:

| Asset class | Minimum | Target | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equity | 75% | 85% | 95% |
| Fixed income | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| Cash | 0% | 3% | 5% |

3.2 The Firm will rebalance when an asset class moves outside its range, and will generally use new contributions and proceeds from Kestrel share sales to do so.

## 4. Concentration

4.1 **Single issuer.** No single issuer may exceed 10% of the market value of the Portfolio, except for Kestrel Semiconductor, which is governed by Section 4.2. For this limit, exposure is measured on direct holdings only. Diversified mutual funds and ETFs are not looked through.

4.2 **Kestrel Semiconductor.** Because a large share of the Patels' net worth and future income depends on Kestrel, the Firm will reduce the Kestrel position to below 15% of the market value of the Portfolio by December 31, 2028. The Firm will sell at least one quarter of the Kestrel shares held at the start of each calendar year, subject to Kestrel's trading windows and any blackout periods that apply to Priya. No additional Kestrel shares will be purchased in the Portfolio. Shares received through the vesting of Priya's equity awards are permitted.

4.3 **Individual stocks.** Individual stocks other than Kestrel may not exceed 20% of the market value of the Portfolio in aggregate.

## 5. Investment Restrictions

5.1 The Portfolio will not hold cryptocurrency, funds or trusts that hold cryptocurrency, or other digital asset products.

5.2 Fixed income will be held only through diversified bond funds or US Treasury securities.

5.3 The Portfolio should maintain a meaningful allocation to international equities.

## 6. Tax Management

6.1 The Firm will use tax-loss harvesting in the joint account where practical.

6.2 Where possible, bond funds will be held in the IRA.

6.3 The Firm will coordinate Kestrel share sales with the Patels' tax preparer so that sales in any year take into account the tax cost and the Patels' other income.

## 7. Review

7.1 The Firm and the Patels will review the Portfolio and this IPS at least annually, and within 30 days after each Kestrel vesting event.

7.2 The Patels will inform the Firm of any change in employment, income or goals, or in Priya's equity awards.
